Takes a list of primers and a reference FASTA and finds every primer pair that would amplify a product up to a chosen length, at a given annealing temperature and salt concentration. It reports where each primer binds and with how many mismatches, the amplicon coordinates, and the melting temperatures of amplicons and primer dimers.
