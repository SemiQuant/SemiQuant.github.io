Non-polyadenylated RNAs make up a large part of the transcriptome and include many regulators of gene expression. selSeq selectively removes mRNA and pre-mRNA from a sample, so that long non-coding RNA, enhancer RNA and non-canonical mRNA can be sequenced. Published in PLOS ONE (2023).
