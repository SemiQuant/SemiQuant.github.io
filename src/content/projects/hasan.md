Allele frequencies say how much of each resistance mutation is in a sample, but not whether two mutations sit in the same bacteria. Hasan reconstructs haplotypes for drug-resistant subpopulations of *Mycobacterium tuberculosis* from Illumina or nanopore reads. For each drug and drug combination it reports the range of population fractions the reads support, and it says where the reads cannot settle the question.
